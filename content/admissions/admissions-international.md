## What do international applicants need?

The Bachelor entry requirements page gives international students the same process as transfer cases: transcripts authenticated by the issuing institution, evaluation of credits by the Deans of the faculties, and admission by the Admissions Office (admissions@ictuniversity.edu.cm). International students pay tuition into the dedicated international accounts listed in the payment channels document. Visa support is available as a request in the ERP (visa-support letter under Requests and Services). The website does not describe certificate equivalence rules.
