## How long does the Masters program take?

The BMS Master's programs (Applied Statistics and Demography, Development Planning, iMBA) last 2 years and 132 credits, with a capstone (organizational internship or industrial training, dissertation, business plan). The ICT Master's programs (Artificial Intelligence, Applied ICTs in Education, Applied ICT in Public Health, Information Systems Security, Information Technology, Information Systems and Networking) are delivered online or onsite in English or French; the Information Systems and Networking page lists intakes in Fall, Spring and Summer and a capstone of internship, thesis and business plan.
