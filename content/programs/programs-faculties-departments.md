## Which faculties exist and what do they offer?

Faculty of Information and Communication Technology (ICT): Bachelor programs in Computer Science, Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Digital Agriculture, Digital Media and Mass Communication, ICT, Information Systems and Networking, Renewable Energy and Software Engineering; Master's programs in Artificial Intelligence, Applied ICTs in Education, Applied ICT in Public Health, Information Systems Security, Information Technology and Information Systems and Networking; short courses and professional tracks. The faculty page lists laboratories (Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Multimedia, Networking, Programming, Robotics and IoT) and an Innovation and Entrepreneurship Hub.

Faculty of Business Management and Sustainability (BMS): Bachelor programs in Accounting, Banking and Finance and Business Management and Sustainability; Master's programs in Applied Statistics and Demography, Development Planning and the International Master in Business Administration.

Deans and heads of department are not named on the website.
