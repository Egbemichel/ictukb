## How long is a Bachelor's degree and what is awarded?

Bachelor programs last 4 years (8 semesters) and lead to the Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree; the Digital Media and Mass Communication page states a Bachelor of Arts. The BMS faculty page states a total of 240 credits, a minimum GPA of 2.00, instruction in English plus 4 credits of a second language. BMS programs such as Accounting, Banking and Finance and Business Management include an industrial attachment (8 weeks for Banking and Finance and Business Management).
