## Where do I see my results on the ERP?

Open My Results. It lists every course with the continuous assessment mark (CA, out of 30), the exam mark (out of 70), the total (out of 100), the grade and the grade points, semester by semester. A one-page Result Slip can be printed for any semester from the same page. The dashboard also shows your CGPA.

## Why can I not see my results?

Only results approved by the Dean or the Registrar appear. Results are also hidden while you have outstanding fees; paying the fees (and having the receipt verified) unlocks them. A moratorium granted by Finance lets you sit exams despite a balance but never unlocks results. If a course shows Withheld, the university has held that result back for a reason shown with it, together with the office to contact.

## What if a mark is wrong or missing?

Raise a complaint from the Complaints page for that course and mark type. Each mark type gets its own ticket. See the complaints document for the steps.
