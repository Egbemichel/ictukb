## Where is the university located?

The ICT University campus is in Yaounde, Cameroon, at Dispensaire Messassi Avenue (P.O. Box 526), as printed on the letterhead of official university notices of November 2026. The university also has a U.S. foundation office in Baton Rouge, Louisiana (P.O. Box 86236).

## What are the contact details for the campus?

See the contacts document: telephone +237 678 76 40 37 and email admin@ictuniversity.edu.cm from the university website.

The campus appears on Google Maps as "ICT University, Messassi, Zoatupsi, Yaounde": https://maps.app.goo.gl/kW7vsVr9yCxigMU5A (link provided by the university community).
