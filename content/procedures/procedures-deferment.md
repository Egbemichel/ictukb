## How do I defer, withdraw or request leave?

Under Requests and Services in the ERP choose name correction, leave of absence, deferment, withdrawal or reinstatement, give the reason and the date requested, and submit. The Registrar's Office processes the request and you follow its status and reference number on the page.

## How do I change my program?

Open Change of Major in the ERP and request a move to another programme of the same degree type (BSc or MSc), with a reason. Only one request can be pending at a time. Your current Coordinator reviews your record and decides. If approved, your programme changes everywhere immediately and ungraded registrations under the old programme are dropped.
