## How do I file a complaint about a mark or a grade?

Complaints about results are filed online in the ICTU ERP; nobody needs to queue at an office. Open the Complaints page (More, then Complaints on a computer; Menu, then Complaints on a phone), keep "About a Course" selected, choose the course, and click the category that fits: Missing CA Marks, Missing Exam Marks, Missing Resit Marks, Wrong Mark Entered, Grade Appeal (a request to re-mark the script), Attendance Dispute, or Other. For a missing or wrong mark, tick the mark concerned (CA out of 30, exam and resit out of 70) and type the mark you expect. Describe the problem in a few precise sentences, optionally attach evidence (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB), then click Submit Complaint. The ERP gives a ticket number such as C-2026-811; keep it. You cannot open a second complaint on the same course and category while the first is open; reply to the open one instead.

## Who sees my name?

For exam marks, resit marks and grade appeals the lecturer sees only your exam code, so do not write your name or matricule in the description or on the evidence. For CA marks, attendance and other complaints the lecturer sees your name.

## What happens to my complaint, and how long does it take?

A course complaint goes to the course lecturer, who corrects the mark, asks a question, or closes the complaint with a reason. A corrected mark must be approved by the Dean or the Registrar before it appears on your results and transcript. Each person handling a complaint has a response deadline; when it passes, the faculty is reminded and the Coordinator or the Admin Assistant can handle the complaint. The guide does not state a number of days. You can follow the status in My Complaints (Submitted, Being handled, Awaiting approval, Resolved or Closed).

## Can I appeal?

Yes, once. Open the resolved or closed complaint, explain why it should be looked at again under "Not satisfied with the outcome?", and click Send appeal. Give every reason and all evidence, because only one appeal is allowed.

## How do I file a complaint about a service?

A problem that is not about the marks of one course (a timetable clash, a registration problem, a service you did not receive) is a general complaint. On the Complaints page click General Complaint, choose whether to send it to the Admin Assistant, your Coordinator or the Dean, write a subject, describe the issue, optionally attach evidence, and click Submit Complaint.

## What is not a complaint?

If results are hidden because tuition is unpaid, results appear once the semester's tuition is cleared; contact the Finance office. If a course shows Withheld, contact the office shown with the reason.
