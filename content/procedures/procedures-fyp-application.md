## How is the senior (final year) project handled in the ERP?

Open Senior Project in the ERP. You propose topics and a supervisor, work with your supervisor in the project workspace (documents and chat), upload your report for validation, and follow its review by the Scientific Council, your defense and the final sign-off. Senior project fees are listed in My Fees.
