1 Before You Complain
A complaint is how you ask the university to look again at a mark or a grade. Every complaint is
handled inside the ICTU ERP: you file it online, you follow it step by step, and you are told when
something happens. Nobody needs to queue at an office.
1.1 Check your results first
Open My Results from the menu. Your results page lists every course of the semester with its
continuous assessment (CA) mark out of 30, its exam mark out of 70, the total out of 100, the grade
and its status.
Figure 1: My Results: each course with its CA, exam, total and grade. Here the exam mark of CSC1222
shows 0, which the student believes is wrong.
Look carefully for:
• a CA or exam mark that is missing (shown as a dash) or shows 0 although you took the
assessment;
• a mark that is different from the one you were told or expected;
• a resit mark that does not appear after you sat the resit.

NOTE: If your results page says your results are hidden because of unpaid fees, this is not
a complaint: results appear once the tuition for the semester is cleared. Go to My Fees or
contact the Finance office.
NOTE: If a course shows Withheld, the university has held that result back for a reason that
is shown with it, together with the office to contact. Contact that office rather than filing a
complaint.
1.2 What you need
• The course concerned, and what exactly is wrong.
• The mark you expected, if you know it.
• Evidence if you have it (optional): a photo or scan of a marked script, a test sheet, an
attendance slip. Accepted formats are PDF, JPG and PNG, up to 10 MB.
CAUTION: For exam and resit marks and for grade appeals, the lecturer sees you only under
your exam code, never your name. Do not write your name or matricule in your description
or on your evidence, so that your script stays anonymous.

2.2 General complaints
A problem that is not about the marks of one course (a timetable clash, a registration problem,
a service you did not receive) is a general complaint. You send it to the Admin Assistant, your
Coordinator or the Dean.

3 Filing a Complaint Step by Step
3.1 Open the Complaints page
On a computer, open More → Complaints in the menu on the left. On a phone, tap Menu at
the bottom of the screen and then Complaints.
Figure 2: The Complaints page: the form to submit a complaint on the left, and your complaints on the
right.
3.2 Fill in the form
1 Keep About a Course selected (it is the default).
2 Choose the Course. Your current marks on record for that course appear under it, so you
can check them before you continue.
3 Click the category that matches your problem. A short explanation of the category appears
under the buttons.
4 For missing or wrong marks, tick the mark concerned (CA, Exam or Resit) and type the mark
you expect: CA out of 30, exam and resit out of 70.
5 Describe the issue in a few clear sentences: when and where you took the assessment, what
you see, and what you expected.

6 Attach your evidence if you have any (optional).
7 Check the line Your complaint will be filed as, then click Submit Complaint.

RECOMMENDED: A good description is short and precise, for example: “I sat the

5 When Your Complaint Is Resolved
5.1 Resolved or closed
When the Dean or the Registrar approves a correction, the complaint becomes Resolved and the
new mark appears on your results and on your transcript. If the staff found that the mark was
right, the complaint is Closed with the reason in the conversation.
Figure 8: A resolved complaint: every step of the tracker is complete, and the box at the bottom lets you
appeal once if you disagree.
5.2 Appealing once
If you disagree with the outcome, you can appeal once:
1 Open the resolved or closed complaint.
2 Under Not satisfied with the outcome?, explain why it should be looked at again.

7 Quick Answers
How long does it take?
The member of staff handling your complaint has a deadline to respond. A mark correction then
needs the approval of the Dean or the Registrar. You can see at every moment where your complaint
is.
Will the lecturer know who I am?
Not for exam marks, resit marks and grade appeals: they see only your exam code. For CA marks,
attendance and other complaints they see your name, because they need it to check their records.
Do I have to attach evidence?
No. Evidence is optional, but it helps the staff decide quickly.
My lecturer is not responding. What do I do?
Nothing extra: when the deadline passes the faculty is reminded, and the Coordinator or the Admin
Assistant can handle your complaint.
Can I complain about several marks at once?
Yes. Tick every mark concerned in the form; each gets its own ticket.
When does my result change?
Only after the Dean or the Registrar approves the correction. Then it appears on your results and
transcript automatically.
My results are hidden. Is that a complaint?
No. Results are hidden until the semester’s tuition is paid. Contact the Finance office.
RECOMMENDED: Check your results as soon as they are published, file your complaint
early, write a clear description, and follow it on the Complaints page until it is resolved.
